Call layouts - mockups

Static mockups for a new call-stage layout system in awful.chat (Svelte 5 PWA; the stage is frontend/src/lib/components/VoiceVideoCallView.svelte). Names, screens and the app are stand-ins. Everything below is drawn by the script at the end of this file; open it in a browser.

The brief

Today: one equal grid of tiles (cameras, screen shares, app tiles), columns by count. Clicking a tile pins it big with a thin thumbnail column - but when the pinned tile is a screen share the thumbnails are switched off, so every camera disappears. A grid menu can hide people without video, shares or apps. Nothing makes a share big on its own.

Proposed: one Layout menu (button at the right of the control bar) with three layouts, a camera strip, and filters.

  • Auto (default): only cameras → grid. Someone shares a screen or opens an app → that goes big, cameras move to a strip. Back to the grid when it ends.
  • Spotlight: one big tile - what you pinned, else whoever is speaking (with a short hold so it does not flicker; SPEAKER_TAKEOVER_MS in spotlight.ts) - plus the strip.
  • Grid: everyone the same size; shares and apps are tiles too.
  • Camera strip: side when the stage is wide, bottom when narrow (or as chosen), or hidden; sizes S/M/L; speakers first; overflow collapses into a "+N more people" tile. Cameras not on screen are not downloaded (the stage already only pulls visible remote cameras).
  • Show: people All / With video / None, screen shares, apps, your own camera (hide self view).
  • Two shares sit side by side in the big area.
  • Your own share is shown big (decided), with a "You're presenting · Stop sharing" bar on it.
  • Choices are remembered per device. No host-only features (awful.chat has no host).

Phases: 1) Auto / Spotlight / Grid, strip side/bottom, shares go big automatically, cameras stay visible next to a pinned share, persistence. 2) Strip size and a draggable edge, hide self view, two shares side by side. 3) Several pins at once, drag to reorder.
