THIRD PARTY NOTICES
===================

awful.chat bundles third party open source software. This file reproduces the
license texts and attribution notices required by those licenses.

The noise suppression that runs on your microphone ships as a prebuilt
WebAssembly + AudioWorklet bundle at /audio-worklet.js. It is built from a fork
of DataDog's dtln-rs-demo, which wraps the dtln-rs module. Both notices follow.

Sources:
  https://github.com/DataDog/dtln-rs
  https://github.com/DataDog/dtln-rs-demo
  https://github.com/FlavioZanoni/dtln-rs-web    (the fork this build uses)

The DTLN approach these implement was published as "Dual-Signal Transformation
LSTM Network for Real-Time Noise Suppression":
  https://github.com/breizhn/DTLN
